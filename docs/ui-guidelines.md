## Visual Direction

- Light theme only for the hackathon prototype.
- Bright, clean, optimistic visual style.
- White or very light warm-grey page background.
- Strong dark text for projector readability.
- Use bright accent colors rather than large dark surfaces.
- Avoid the visual language of monitoring/surveillance dashboards.
- Avoid dense banking-enterprise UI.
- Coaching should feel supportive, calm, and personal.

## Color Usage

- Bright blue / indigo as the primary action color.
- Fresh teal or green for positive progress / strengths.
- Warm coral or orange for coaching attention and nudges.
- Very pale tinted backgrounds for cards.
- Never rely on low-contrast grey text.

## Layout

- Large typography.
- Generous spacing.
- Few elements per screen.
- Large cards and buttons.
- Information should be readable from several metres away during a demo.
- Avoid tables unless absolutely necessary.
- Avoid large analytics dashboards and complicated charts.

## Coaching UI

- Live nudges should be visually noticeable but not alarming.
- Do not use red warning/error styling for coaching feedback.
- No numeric empathy scores.
- Use natural language and qualitative progress.
- Focus on one or two important pieces of feedback at a time.
